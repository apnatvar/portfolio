# Positioning

## Current position

Apnatva Singh Rawat (AP) is an India-based, remote web product engineer focused on technical SEO and implementation for Next.js sites. The service set covers technical SEO, programmatic SEO, SEO automation, and Core Web Vitals. Next.js SEO is a capability within technical SEO, not a separate primary service.

The practical distinction to emphasize is the connection between diagnosis and implementation: inspect the route, template, data, or workflow behind a problem; prioritize the changes; implement or hand over verifiable work. A small-product focus and reusable checks are positioning hypotheses to test, not proven differentiators.

## Audience and job

Speak to small product teams, founders, and marketing or engineering leads who have a concrete search or performance issue and need technical work that fits an existing product. Their job is to find the cause, choose a useful next step, and leave with changes or instructions their team can maintain.

## Proof standard

Use public project descriptions only as portfolio evidence of work performed. They are not independently audited case studies and do not establish ranking, traffic, revenue, or performance outcomes. Do not imply guarantees. Describe methods and deliverables; state measured results only when a dated, attributable source is available and approved for publication.

## Boundaries

- Lead with SEO engineering and web performance; describe broader product engineering as supporting experience.
- Do not claim a published `/skills` section or route.
- Do not make up client results, rates, credentials, endorsements, or service guarantees.
- Keep engagement scope and pricing to the project discussion unless current, approved facts are supplied.
