# Global growth roadmap

This is a sequence for learning and improving the site, not a forecast or promise of growth. The site states that AP is based in India and works remotely with teams worldwide.

## 1. Establish a clear global entry point

Keep service descriptions in plain international English. Explain scope, required access, collaboration and handover. Route inquiries through `/hire-ap`; agree on timezone overlap and communication expectations per engagement rather than promising universal availability.

## 2. Make the offer legible

Keep four specialist services distinct and technical SEO inclusive of Next.js SEO. Publish deliverables, fit criteria, process, FAQs, and limitations. Use portfolio items as project evidence without implying independently verified business results.

## 3. Build durable discovery

Publish useful guides mapped to service intent; connect guides, services, tools, and work with contextual links. Maintain canonical and metadata quality. No `/skills` page is published.

## 4. Learn from real demand

Once enough data exists, review Search Console queries and qualified inquiries by geography, language, and service. Record what buyers ask, where scope is unclear, and what work is actually won. Do not infer market size or keyword demand without suitable data.

## 5. Localize only with evidence

Consider a localized page only when there is demonstrated demand, language capability, and a credible local service proposition. Keep translated copy accurate and reviewed; avoid duplicating thin pages for countries without a meaningful difference in offer or delivery.

## Review signals

Use qualified inquiry volume and fit, inquiry-to-engagement progression, and Search Console query/page trends as learning signals. Establish a baseline before choosing targets. Rankings, impressions, and traffic are not guaranteed outcomes.
