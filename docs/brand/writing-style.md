# Writing style

## Voice

Clear, specific, calm, and technically literate. Write for founders, product teams, and marketing or engineering leads. Explain a technical term when it affects a decision.

## Copy rules

- Describe the reader's problem, work performed, deliverable, and boundary in concrete language.
- Prefer active voice and short paragraphs. Use lists for steps, deliverables, and fit criteria.
- Distinguish diagnosis, recommendation, implementation, and measured outcome.
- State uncertainty plainly; do not hide it behind confident marketing language.
- Use “I” for individual work and “we” only when a real team is involved.
- Keep service and guide terminology consistent with the four primary offers.

## Avoid

- Guaranteed rankings, traffic, conversion, or Core Web Vitals scores.
- Unsupported superlatives, client results, credentials, testimonials, or market comparisons.
- Keyword-stuffed headings, generic claims such as “unlock growth,” and promises that automation makes decisions independently.
- A published `/skills` page claim; no such route is published.

## Technical copy

Use current metric names (LCP, INP, CLS) and explain that field results vary by audience, devices, and conditions. Treat lab measurements as diagnostic evidence, not a substitute for real-user data.
