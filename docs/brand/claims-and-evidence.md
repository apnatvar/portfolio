# Claims and evidence

## Standard

Every factual claim about services, experience, projects, metrics, or outcomes must have a source that can be checked. Keep a record of the source and date for claims that may change. If support is absent, remove the claim or describe the work without an outcome assertion.

## Approved source types

- Current portfolio content, code, repositories, and project records for implementation descriptions.
- First-party analytics or Search Console exports for dated search metrics, with scope and measurement window stated.
- Field or lab reports for performance measurements, naming the source and test conditions.
- Education or employment records for credentials and experience dates.
- Client approval for names, logos, testimonials, and non-public project information.

## Required context for outcome claims

State the metric, baseline or comparison, time period, data source, and attribution limitation. Do not claim that technical work alone caused a search or business outcome when other factors could have contributed.

## Prohibited without new evidence

- Guaranteed rankings, traffic, revenue, lead, conversion, or score improvements.
- Invented client names, testimonials, case studies, rates, credentials, or success statistics.
- Competitor prices or performance figures as comparative facts. Competitor pages are self-presentations and should only inform a qualitative summary with direct links.
- Search-volume or difficulty statements absent a suitable keyword dataset. No paid or volume keyword research is currently available.

## Review

Before publishing a new claim, check the source, date, permission, and wording. Recheck time-sensitive technical claims when the relevant platform changes.
